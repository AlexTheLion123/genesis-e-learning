How it starts

Start small, inside your own bank.

A one-month trial with 10 to 15 of your own staff, before anything goes bank-wide. TBC

  1. Choose a group

    Staff from two departments, at head office and in branches, with one senior sponsor.

  2. Run it for a month

    Induction courses, one job-skills course, tests and certificates, with your branding.

  3. Review the results

    How many finished, scores before and after, and ease of use, with a fix list before launch.

No bank-wide commitment is needed to see it working.

FAQ

Questions banks ask us

We already have a learning system. Why choose this?

A learning system is the smaller part. What is hard to get is knowing where your skills gaps are, banking content in Amharic and English to close them, and a clear view of what the training achieved.TBC

Where is our data stored?

We host in Ethiopia and offer in-country data options, designed around Ethiopia's data protection law. TBC

How does it connect to our HR and sign-in systems?

Staff sign in with the bank's own accounts. Employee data comes in through a standard template, with automated options where your IT team wants them.

What happens after I book a demo?

A first conversation where we show the platform and talk through where your skills gaps are. If it makes sense, a working session follows.
